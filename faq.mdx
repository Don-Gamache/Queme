Whether you're joining your first event as a participant or setting up a multi-day competition as a host, this page covers the most common questions about Queme. If you don't find what you're looking for here, reach out to the team directly at [support@queme.io](mailto:support@queme.io).

## Getting Started

<Accordion title="Is Queme free to use?">
  Participants can download and use Queme entirely for free — finding events, following run orders, tracking dogs, and viewing scoreboards costs nothing. Hosts receive **2 free event credits** when they upgrade to a Host account, which is enough to run your first couple of events at no cost. Additional event credits can be purchased inside the app as you need them.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Which devices does Queme support?">
  Queme is available on both major mobile platforms:

  - **iOS** — Download from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/queme-io/id6738308134) for iPhone and iPad.
  - **Android** — Download from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.queme.app) for any Android phone or tablet.

  You can also access Queme through your mobile browser at [queme.io](https://queme.io) if you prefer a web experience.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I use the same account on multiple devices?">
  Yes. Your Queme account is tied to your login credentials, not a single device. Sign in with the same Google, Apple, email, or Facebook account on any phone or tablet and all your followed events, dogs, and preferences will be available immediately.
</Accordion>

## For Participants

<Accordion title="How do I find my event?">
  Open the Queme app and use the **Search** feature on the Events screen to look up your event by name. Event names are set by the host, so if you're having trouble locating it, ask the host for the exact name they used when creating the event. Once you find it, tap **Follow** to add it to your event list and start receiving live updates.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why can't I see the scoreboard?">
  The Live Scoreboard is an optional feature that hosts choose to enable for their event. If the host has not turned on scoring, no scoreboard tab will appear for that event. If you expect to see scores but can't find the scoreboard, check with your event host to confirm whether scoring has been enabled.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I stop notifications for an event?">
  To stop all notifications for an event, open the event in the app and tap **Unfollow**. You'll no longer receive push notifications for run order changes or host announcements tied to that event.

  To stop notifications for a specific dog only, tap the **bell icon** next to that dog's name. Tapping the bell a second time unsubscribes you from that dog's alerts while keeping your event-level notifications active.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What do the dog status labels mean?">
  Queme uses four status labels to give everyone real-time visibility into where each dog is in the run order:

  | Status | Meaning |
  |---|---|
  | **Checked In** | The dog has arrived at the venue and been checked in by the host. |
  | **In Line** | The dog is queued up and waiting for its turn to run. |
  | **Running** | The dog is currently on course competing in its run. |
  | **Completed** | The dog has finished its run for that class or round. |

  Statuses are updated by the host or check-in staff and refresh live for all followers.
</Accordion>

## For Hosts

<Accordion title="How long can an event run?">
  A single Queme event can span **up to 7 days**, making it suitable for weekend trials, multi-day competitions, and extended club events. You set the start and end dates when you create the event, and participants can follow the event for its entire duration.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How many runs can I add to an event?">
  You can add **unlimited runs** to any event. Whether you're running a single ring trial or a large multi-ring competition with dozens of classes, there's no cap on the number of runs you can build inside a single event.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I import dogs from a spreadsheet?">
  Yes. Inside any run, use the **Excel Import** feature to bulk-add dogs from a spreadsheet file instead of entering them one by one. Format your spreadsheet with the required columns, upload the file, and Queme will populate the run's dog list automatically. This is especially useful for large trials where entry lists already exist in Excel or CSV format.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is a Bark Message?">
  A **Bark Message** is a targeted push notification you send to everyone who is following a specific dog in your event. Unlike a general event notification that goes to all event followers, a Bark Message reaches only the people who have subscribed to updates for that particular dog — making it ideal for dog-specific announcements like "Rover is now on deck" or "Check-in closes in 10 minutes for dogs in Ring 2." You'll find the Bark Message option in the dog management section of each run.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I see the event from a participant's perspective?">
  Yes. Use the **Switch View** feature to toggle between **Host View** and **Participant View** at any time without logging out. Participant View shows you exactly what attendees and followers see — the live run order, dog statuses, scoreboard, and notifications — so you can confirm everything looks correct before and during your event.
</Accordion>

## Account & Privacy

<Accordion title="How do I delete my data?">
  To request deletion of your Queme account and associated data, send an email to [support@queme.io](mailto:support@queme.io) with the subject line **"Data Deletion Request"** and include the email address linked to your account. The Queme team will process your request and remove your data within a reasonable timeframe, in accordance with applicable privacy regulations.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What data does Queme collect?">
  Queme collects the minimum information needed to provide the service, including:

  - **Account information** — your name and email address (from your sign-in method).
  - **Dog names** — entered by you or your host when dogs are added to events.
  - **Approximate device location** — used to surface nearby event recommendations.

  For complete details on how your data is collected, stored, and used, review the full [Privacy Policy](https://queme.io/privacy-policy) at queme.io. If you have specific privacy questions, contact [support@queme.io](mailto:support@queme.io).
</Accordion>
