Queme was born from the dog event community in 2024 — built by people who know the frustration of crowded scoreboards, missed run calls, and disconnected participants. Whether you compete, cheer from the sidelines, or run the whole show, Queme keeps everyone in sync from check-in to final score. The app is available on iOS and Android, so your whole community can join no matter what device they carry.

## Two Roles, One App

Queme is designed around two distinct roles: **Participants** and **Hosts**. Every account starts as a Participant, giving you immediate access to live run orders, dog tracking, and scoreboards. When you're ready to run your own events, upgrading to Host takes just a few taps — and comes with two free event credits to get you started.

### Participants

As a Participant, you can search for and follow any public event on Queme. Once you're following an event, you get live access to run orders and can tap the bell icon next to your dog to receive push notifications the moment its status changes — from Checked In, through In Line and Running, all the way to Completed. A live scoreboard keeps you up to date on results without ever having to push through the crowd.

### Hosts

As a Host, you create and manage events up to seven days long, build unlimited runs, and add dogs manually or by importing an Excel file. You control every dog's check-in status and can push targeted **Bark Messages** directly to a specific dog's followers, or send custom notifications to everyone following an event or a single run. You can switch between Host and Participant views at any time, so you never lose sight of the competitor experience.
