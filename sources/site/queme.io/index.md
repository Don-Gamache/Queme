## Explore Our Key Features

Discover how Queme.io can enhance your dog event experience with our innovative and user-friendly features.

![](https://queme.io/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/20241119-223036000-ios.png)

### Real-Time Updates

Digital run orders allow your event participants to be informed in advance about when they will soon need to take their turn to run. This feature ensures that everyone is prepared and knows exactly when to get ready, enhancing the overall experience of the event.

![](https://queme.io/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/20241119-221937000-ios.png)

### Easy Event Management

Our advanced Check-In system is designed to streamline event management by allowing hosts to efficiently manage attendee status with just a few clicks. Hosts can easily mark attendees as “Checked In” or “Not Yet Checked In,” providing a real-time overview of attendance. This feature not only simplifies the check-in process but also ensures that event organizers can focus on delivering a seamless and engaging experience for all participants.

![](https://queme.io/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/20241224-022800000-ios.png)

### Push Notifications

Push notifications deliver instant alerts to keep everyone informed about any delays, rescheduling of events, or updates regarding order notifications. This efficient communication tool ensures that all participants are kept in the loop, allowing for seamless adjustments and minimizing confusion. Whether you’re managing an event or coordinating orders, timely push notifications are essential for maintaining engagement and clarity.

## The Story Behind Queme.io

QueMe.io started as a simple web-based solution back in 2024, lovingly adopted by the Kinners on 97 community to simplify dog event management. The enthusiasm and feedback from those early adopters inspired us to dream bigger and push further. Over the years, we refined our platform, added new features, and expanded our reach. 
Today, QueMe.io has grown into a full-featured app available on both the Play Store and App Store. Our journey from a web solution to a modern, versatile app reflects our commitment to innovation and our passion for the dog event community. As we continue to grow, our mission remains the same: to make dog event management seamless, enjoyable, and efficient for everyone involved. 
Experience the future of dog event management. Download QueMe.io now and take control of the ring!
