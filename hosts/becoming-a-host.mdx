Every Queme account starts as a participant account, giving you full access to finding events, following dogs, and viewing live run orders. When you're ready to run your own events, upgrading to a Host account unlocks a powerful set of tools — all from within the app. The upgrade takes just a few taps, and new hosts receive **2 free event credits** to get started right away.

## Upgrade to Host

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Queme App">
    Launch Queme on your iOS or Android device and make sure you're signed in to your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Your Profile or Account Settings">
    Tap your profile icon or navigate to the account settings section from the main menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Upgrade to Host">
    Locate the **Upgrade to Host** option and tap it to begin the upgrade process.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the Upgrade">
    Review the details and confirm your upgrade. Your account is instantly elevated to a Host account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive Your Free Credits">
    Once the upgrade is complete, you'll see **2 free event credits** added to your account. Each credit lets you create and run one event.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Understanding Host Credits

Each event credit represents one event you can create and publish in Queme. When you create a new event, one credit is consumed. Your 2 free credits give you the ability to run two full events — with no upfront cost — so you can explore all the host features before committing further.

<Note>
  If you need additional credits beyond your 2 free ones, contact the Queme support team at [support@queme.io](mailto:support@queme.io) or call **314-295-3893** for assistance.
</Note>

## You're Still a Participant Too

Upgrading to a Host account doesn't take away your participant experience. You can switch between Host View and Participant View at any time directly within the app, so you can still follow events, track dogs, and view scoreboards just like any other attendee.

<Note>
  Use the **Switch View** feature in the app to toggle between your Host dashboard and your Participant view. This is especially useful when you want to preview exactly what your attendees see.
</Note>
