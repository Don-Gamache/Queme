Bark Messages are one of Queme's most powerful communication tools for hosts. Unlike a general event announcement that goes to everyone, a Bark Message is a **targeted push notification** sent only to participants who are actively following a specific dog. That means your message lands with exactly the right people — the handler, their family, their friends — without cluttering everyone else's notification feed.

## When to Use Bark Messages

Bark Messages are best suited for dog-specific, action-oriented communication where the recipient needs to act immediately. Common use cases include:

- **"Buddy, you're on deck!"** — alerting a handler that their dog is next in the ring
- **"Please bring Cleo to Ring 2 immediately"** — redirecting a handler to a different location
- **"Fluffy scratched — please check in with the gate steward"** — notifying a handler of an administrative issue
- **"Great run, Luna!"** — a personal congratulations to the handler and their supporters

## Send a Bark Message

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Your Run">
    From your Host dashboard, navigate to the event and tap the run that contains the dog you want to message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Dog">
    Scroll through the run's dog list to locate the specific dog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Send Bark Message">
    Tap on the dog's entry to open the dog options, then tap **Send Bark Message**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type Your Message">
    Write a short, clear message in the text field. Focus on one specific action or piece of information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Tap **Send**. The message is delivered instantly as a push notification to every participant who has tapped the bell icon for that dog.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use Bark Messages for urgent, dog-specific instructions where clarity and immediacy matter. Because the message goes only to that dog's followers, it cuts through the noise of general event announcements and is far more likely to be acted on quickly.
</Tip>

## Bark Messages vs. Event Notifications

It's important to understand when to use a Bark Message versus a broader event or run notification:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Bark Messages">
    - Sent to followers of **one specific dog**
    - Best for handler-specific instructions or updates
    - Delivers the highest relevance to the recipient
    - Found inside the individual dog entry within a run
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Event & Run Notifications">
    - Sent to all followers of an **event** or a **run**
    - Best for general announcements — delays, schedule changes, venue updates
    - Reaches a broader audience
    - Found at the event or run level in your Host dashboard
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
  Keep Bark Messages short and actionable. A message like "Penny — Ring 3, you're next!" is far more effective than a long explanation. Participants receive the message as a push notification, so they see it at a glance — make it count.
</Note>
