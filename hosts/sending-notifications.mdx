When you need to reach a broad group of participants at once, Queme's notification system lets you send announcements to everyone following your event or a specific run. These broad notifications are the right tool for general updates — schedule changes, weather delays, venue directions, or any information that applies to a large group of attendees. For messages aimed at a single dog's handler or supporters, use [Bark Messages](/hosts/bark-messages) instead.

## Two Types of Broad Notifications

Queme gives hosts two scopes for sending notifications:

- **Event-Level Notifications** — sent to all participants who follow your event. Use these for announcements that affect the entire competition, such as a delayed start, a venue change, or a reminder about general rules.
- **Run-Level Notifications** — sent to all participants who follow a specific run. Use these for updates that are relevant to one ring or class, such as a ring change, a temporary pause, or a class-specific schedule update.

## Send an Event-Level Notification

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Your Event">
    From your Host dashboard, tap the event you want to send a notification for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Notifications or Announcements">
    Within your event, tap the **Notifications** or **Announcements** option. This is your event-wide broadcast tool.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compose Your Message">
    Write a clear, informative message. Include any key details participants need — times, locations, or specific actions they should take.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Your Audience">
    Confirm that the audience is set to **Event Followers** — all participants who have followed the event.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Tap **Send**. The notification is delivered immediately as a push notification to every event follower.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Send a Run-Level Notification

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Specific Run">
    From your event view, tap the run you want to send a notification for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Notify">
    Inside the run, tap the **Notify** or **Send Notification** option.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compose Your Message">
    Write a message relevant to this run — for example, "Ring 2 will be delayed by approximately 20 minutes" or "Height order has changed — 20" class will now run before 16"."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send to Run Followers">
    Confirm the audience is set to followers of this run, then tap **Send**. Only participants following that specific run will receive the notification.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  For alerts targeted at a single dog or handler — such as calling a specific dog to the ring — use [Bark Messages](/hosts/bark-messages) instead of a run or event notification. Bark Messages reach only that dog's followers, making the alert far more relevant and actionable for the recipient.
</Tip>

## Notification Reach and Best Practices

<Note>
  Participants who haven't followed your event or a specific run will **not** receive these notifications. Remind your attendees at registration or on-site to download Queme and follow the event so they don't miss any announcements. A quick instruction at the gate — "Follow our event in the Queme app for live run orders and updates" — goes a long way.
</Note>

<Accordion title="What if my notification isn't going through?">

</Accordion>
