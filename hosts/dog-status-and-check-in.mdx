Queme's status system is the heartbeat of your live event experience. As dogs move through your competition day — from arriving at the venue to completing their run — you update their status in the app, and everyone following that dog sees the change instantly. This keeps participants, handlers, and spectators informed without any announcements over a loudspeaker or back-and-forth phone calls.

## The Status Workflow

Every dog in a run moves through the following statuses in sequence:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Not Yet Checked In** | The dog has been added to the run but hasn't been verified at the event. |
| **Checked In** | The dog and handler have arrived and been confirmed at the venue. |
| **In Line** | The dog is staged and ready to run — in the queue or warm-up area. |
| **Running** | The dog is actively on course or in the ring right now. |
| **Completed** | The dog has finished its run. A score can optionally be recorded. |

## Check In a Dog

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Run">
    From your Host dashboard, navigate to the event and tap the run containing the dog you want to check in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Dog">
    Scroll through the dog list to locate the dog. Dogs with a status of **Not Yet Checked In** are displayed in the main list by default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap to Mark as Checked In">
    Tap the dog's entry and select **Checked In** to confirm the dog's arrival. The status updates immediately across all participant views.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Revert If Needed">
    If you check in a dog by mistake, tap the dog again and select **Not Yet Checked In** to revert the status.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Update Statuses During the Event

As your event progresses, keep each dog's status current by advancing it through the workflow.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Move a Dog to In Line">
    When a dog is called to the staging area or warm-up ring, tap the dog and select **In Line**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark the Dog as Running">
    The moment the dog enters the ring or begins its run, update the status to **Running**. Followers watching remotely see this update in real time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move to Completed">
    When the run finishes, tap the dog and select **Completed**. The dog moves to the **Completed** tab in the run view, clearing it from the active queue so you can focus on the dogs still running.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optionally Add a Score">
    When marking a dog as Completed, you have the option to enter a score. Scores are displayed on the live scoreboard that participants can view in the app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check-In Overview

Beyond individual run management, Queme provides a **Check-In Overview** for your event — an at-a-glance attendance list showing which dogs across all runs have checked in and which haven't. Use this before each run begins to confirm you have a complete picture of who's on-site.

<Tip>
  Keep statuses updated as close to real time as possible. Participants who can't be ringside — watching from home, waiting in their car, or at another ring — rely entirely on these status changes to know when to head over. Even a 30-second update delay can make a meaningful difference for a handler rushing between rings.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Every status change triggers a **push notification** to all participants who are following that specific dog via the bell icon. This means you don't need to manually alert anyone — the app handles it the moment you tap the update.
</Note>
