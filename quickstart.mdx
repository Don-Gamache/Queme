Whether you're heading to a dog event this weekend or you're the one putting it together, Queme gets you set up fast. Participants can find an event, follow a run, and start tracking their dog in just a few taps. Hosts can create a full event with runs and dogs in under ten minutes. Pick your path below and follow the steps.

## Choose Your Path

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="I'm a Participant">
    As a Participant, your goal is to follow an event and get live alerts when your dog is called. Here's how to get there from a fresh install:

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Download Queme">
        Install Queme from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/queme-io/id6738308134) or [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.queme.app). It's free to download.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Sign In">
        Open the app and sign in with Google, Apple, Email, or Facebook. Your account is created automatically on your first sign-in.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Find Your Event">
        Tap the **Search** or **Events** section from the home screen. Enter the event name or browse upcoming events near you. Tap the event to open its detail page.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Tap Follow">
        On the event detail page, tap **Follow**. This subscribes you to the event and unlocks the live run order view, scoreboard, and dog status updates for that event.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Open the Run">
        Inside the event, tap the run your dog is entered in to open the live run order list. You'll see each dog listed with its current status: **Checked In**, **In Line**, **Running**, or **Completed**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Tap the Bell Icon Next to Your Dog">
        Find your dog in the run order and tap the **bell icon** beside its name. Queme will now send you a push notification every time your dog's status changes — so you never miss your turn in the ring.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Tip>
      You can follow as many events and runs as you like, and track multiple dogs across different runs. Each bell subscription is independent.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="I'm a Host">
    As a Host, you'll create the event, build the runs, add the dogs, and keep participants updated throughout the day. Here's the full flow from a fresh install:

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Download Queme">
        Install Queme from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/queme-io/id6738308134) or [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.queme.app). It's free to download.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Sign In">
        Open the app and sign in with Google, Apple, Email, or Facebook. You start as a Participant by default.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Upgrade to Host">
        Navigate to your profile or account settings and tap **Upgrade to Host**. The upgrade comes with **2 free event credits** — no payment information required to get started.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Use Your Free Credits">
        Each event you create costs one credit. Your two free credits let you run two full events before you need to purchase additional credits. Credits never expire.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Create Your Event">
        Tap **Create Event** and fill in the event details: name, location, dates (up to 7 days), and any description you want participants to see. Publish when you're ready to make it searchable.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add Runs">
        Inside your event, tap **Add Run** to create individual runs (for example, different classes or rings). You can build as many runs as you need — there's no limit.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add Dogs">
        Populate each run with dogs. You can add dogs **manually** one at a time, or save time by importing a list from an **Excel file**. Each dog entry includes the dog's name and handler information.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Go Live and Manage the Day">
        Once your runs are populated, your event is live for participants to find and follow. During the event, use the Host view to:
        - Update each dog's status (Checked In → In Line → Running → Completed)
        - Send a **Bark Message** to alert a specific dog's followers
        - Send a **Custom Notification** to all followers of an event or run
        - Switch to **Participant view** anytime to see the experience your competitors see
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Warning>
      Events can span up to 7 days. Make sure your event start and end dates are correct before publishing — participants will see these dates when they search for your event.
    </Warning>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
