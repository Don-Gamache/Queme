Getting into Queme takes just a few minutes. Download the app from the store that matches your device, pick a sign-in method, and you'll land in your account ready to follow events or build your first one. Queme is also accessible on the web at [queme.io](https://queme.io) if you ever need to check in from a browser.

## Create Your Account and Sign In

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Queme App">
    Launch Queme after installing it. You'll land on the welcome screen where you can choose how to sign in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Sign-In Method">
    Queme supports four sign-in options. Tap the one that fits you best:

    - **Sign in with Google** — uses your existing Google account; no new password needed.
    - **Sign in with Apple** — uses your Apple ID; ideal on iPhone and iPad.
    - **Sign in with Email** — enter an email address and create a password directly in Queme.
    - **Sign in with Facebook** — links your Facebook account for a one-tap login.

    Whichever method you choose, Queme creates one unified account tied to that identity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow Push Notifications">
    When prompted, tap **Allow** to enable push notifications. Queme uses notifications to alert you the moment your dog's status changes in a run order — this is the core of the real-time experience.

    <Note>
      You must allow push notifications to receive live run order alerts. If you accidentally deny the prompt, go to your device's **Settings → Notifications → Queme** and enable them manually.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="You're In">
    After signing in, you land on your Queme home screen as a Participant. From here you can search for events, follow runs, and track your dog — or upgrade to Host if you plan to manage events.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Helpful Tips

<Tip>
  Your Queme account works on both iOS and Android simultaneously. Sign in with the same method on any device and your followed events, dogs, and settings carry over automatically.
</Tip>

<Info>
  Queme accounts are free to create. The only paid feature is the Host upgrade, which comes with two free event credits so you can try it at no cost.
</Info>
