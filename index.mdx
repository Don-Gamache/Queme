## How Queme Works

Queme connects two groups of people at every dog event: **participants** who want to know where they are in the order, and **hosts** who need to keep everything running smoothly.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the app">
    Install Queme from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/queme-io/id6738308134) or [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.queme.app) and sign in with your Google, Apple, or email account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your event">
    Browse or search for your event inside the app, then tap **Follow** to subscribe to live updates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track your dog">
    Open the run your dog is entered in and tap the **bell icon** next to their name. You'll receive instant push notifications as they move through the order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stay in the loop">
    Watch live status updates — Checked In, In Line, Running, Completed — and view scores as the host adds them.
  </Step>
</Steps>
