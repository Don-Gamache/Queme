</Note>

## Common Reasons to Contact Support

You're in the right place if you need help with any of the following:

- **Credits & billing** — questions about Host upgrade pricing, purchasing additional event credits, or billing discrepancies.
- **Technical issues** — app crashes, login problems, notifications not delivering, or anything else that isn't working as expected.
- **Feature questions** — not sure how to use Excel import, Bark Messages, the Switch View feature, or another part of the app? The team can walk you through it.
- **Account deletion requests** — to have your account and associated data removed, email [support@queme.io](mailto:support@queme.io) with "Data Deletion Request" in the subject line and the email address linked to your account.

<Tip>
  For the fastest resolution, include your **account email address** and a clear **description of the issue** — or steps to reproduce it — when you reach out. Screenshots or screen recordings are always helpful and can significantly speed up troubleshooting.
</Tip>

## Feedback & Feature Suggestions

Bug reports and feature suggestions are genuinely welcome. The Queme team actively uses community feedback to shape the product roadmap. If you've spotted something broken or have an idea that would make Queme better for participants or hosts, submit it through the official feedback form at [queme.io/feedback](https://queme.io/feedback).
