The live scoreboard in Queme lets you follow the competitive standings of any event as they develop throughout the day. Scores are entered by the host as each dog completes their run, and the scoreboard refreshes instantly — giving every follower an up-to-the-moment view of who's leading without anyone having to step away from the ring to check a whiteboard or wait for a posted sheet.

## How to Access the Scoreboard

The scoreboard is built into the event view. Once you've followed an event (see [Find and Follow Events](/participants/find-and-follow-events)), open the event and look for the **Scoreboard** tab or section within the event detail screen. Tap it to see the current standings for each run or class the host has enabled scoring for.

<Note>
  The Scoreboard tab only appears if the host has chosen to enable the scoring feature for their event. Not all events use Queme's scoring tools — some hosts may manage scores externally or not publish them at all.
</Note>

## How Scores Are Added

Hosts enter scores manually as dogs finish their runs. Each score is tied to a specific dog and run, and appears on the scoreboard as soon as the host submits it. There is no batch upload or delayed posting — scores go live the moment they are recorded.

## Live Updates — No Refresh Required

The scoreboard updates in real time. As the host adds or adjusts scores during the event, your view reflects those changes automatically. You don't need to pull down to refresh or navigate away and back — just leave the scoreboard open and watch the standings shift as the competition progresses.

## Scores and the Completed Tab

When a dog finishes their run, the host moves them to the **Completed** tab in the run list (see [Track Your Dog](/participants/tracking-your-dog)). If scoring is enabled, the dog's score may be visible directly in that completed entry as well as on the overall scoreboard, giving you two places to find results.

<Tip>
  If you don't see a scoreboard inside the event view, the host has not enabled the scoring feature for this event. Reach out to the host directly if you're expecting scores to be posted — they may be using a different system or plan to post results after the event concludes.
</Tip>
