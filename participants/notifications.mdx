Queme uses push notifications to keep you connected to everything happening at an event — without requiring you to constantly check the app. Notifications are delivered at two distinct levels: one for followers of the overall event, and one for followers of a specific dog. Understanding the difference helps you stay informed about exactly what matters to you and avoid alert fatigue.

## Two Levels of Notifications

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Event-Level Notifications" icon="calendar-circle-exclamation" href="/participants/find-and-follow-events">
    Sent to everyone who follows the event. These cover schedule changes, delays, and any general announcements the host broadcasts to all attendees.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Dog-Level Notifications" icon="bell" href="/participants/tracking-your-dog">
    Sent only to participants who tapped the bell icon next to a specific dog. These include status changes and targeted Bark Messages from the host.

## Event-Level Notification Triggers

When you follow an event, you automatically receive push notifications for anything the host sends to the full event audience. Common triggers include:

- **Schedule delays** — the host posts an update that the start time or a run is being pushed back
- **Schedule changes** — run order adjustments, ring reassignments, or class cancellations
- **General announcements** — welcome messages, weather notices, parking updates, or any custom notification the host sends to all event followers

## Dog-Level Notification Triggers

Dog-level notifications are more targeted. You receive them only for dogs you've subscribed to via the bell icon. They fire when:

- **A dog's status changes** — for example, when a dog moves from Checked In to In Line, or from In Line to Running, you receive an alert so you know it's time to head to the ring
- **The host sends a Bark Message** — a Bark Message is a targeted alert the host directs specifically at the followers of a particular dog, such as a last-minute course note or a direct callout to that dog's handler

## Managing Notification Preferences

Queme delivers notifications through your device's native push notification system. To receive alerts, you must have notifications enabled for Queme in your device settings.

**On iOS:**
Open **Settings → Queme → Notifications** and make sure **Allow Notifications** is turned on. You can also choose your preferred alert style (banners, sounds, badges) from this screen.

**On Android:**
Open **Settings → Apps → Queme → Notifications** and enable notifications. Depending on your Android version, you may also be able to manage notification channels to fine-tune which alert types come through.

<Warning>
  If Queme notifications are disabled in your device settings, you will not receive any alerts — even if you follow an event or tap the bell icon on a dog. Always verify your device-level notification permissions are turned on before arriving at an event.
</Warning>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<Accordion title="Why am I not receiving notifications?">
  There are a few common reasons notifications may not be coming through:

  - **Device settings are off** — Go to your device's app notification settings and confirm that Queme is allowed to send notifications. This is the most common cause.
  - **You haven't followed the event** — Event-level notifications only go to followers. Open the event and make sure you've tapped the **Follow** button.
  - **You haven't tapped the bell icon** — Dog-level notifications only go to participants who have subscribed to that specific dog. Find the dog in the run list and tap the bell icon next to their name.
  - **Poor connectivity at the venue** — Notifications require an active internet connection to be delivered. If the venue has limited cell service, alerts may arrive late or be delayed.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I stop notifications for an event?">
  You have two options depending on what you want to stop:

  - **Stop all event-level notifications** — Open the event in Queme and tap **Unfollow**. You'll immediately stop receiving any host broadcasts or schedule updates for that event.
  - **Stop notifications for a specific dog** — Open the run containing that dog, find their entry, and tap the bell icon again to toggle it off. The bell will no longer be highlighted, and you'll stop receiving status alerts and Bark Messages for that dog.

  You can mix and match these options — for example, you can unfollow the overall event while keeping the bell active on your own dog so you still get run-order alerts for them specifically.
</Accordion>
